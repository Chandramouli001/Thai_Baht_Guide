All Thai Currency
=======

The Thai Baht (THB) is the official currency of Thailand, subdivided into 100 satangs, and commonly denoted by ฿. As of early 2026, 1 THB is approximately ₹2.30 to ₹2.40 INR, with banknotes in ฿20, ฿50, ฿100, ฿500, and ฿1000 denominations.
